03 / COMPLIANCE

Keep the evidence traceable.

The result is a retrospective, gross-only research finding—not a live strategy signal.

Historical source

WRDS / CRSP daily data, 2022–2025. The stocks were selected in 2026.

Method

Equal initial allocations; each stock is held when its prior session return is positive, otherwise its sleeve holds cash. No cross-sleeve rebalancing.

Output

Derived aggregate results are loaded from a local CSV. The quote-based cost estimate is blocked; gross is not net.

Quote source

Twelve Data snapshots are fetched separately. They do not update the fixed historical backtest. Quote date and retrieval time are different fields.

Reproducibility

Source data and calculation code are retained in WRDS. A clean-machine cold start and complete independent reproduction still need verification.

These are research controls and limitations, not a representation of legal or regulatory compliance.

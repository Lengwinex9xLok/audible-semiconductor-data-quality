# Why the broader question is contested

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a literature context note, not evidence that the
June daily rule has a persistent edge or loses money in other samples.

[Frazzini, Israel and Moskowitz, Trading Costs of Asset Pricing Anomalies](https://www.aqr.com/insights/research/working-paper/trading-costs-of-asset-pricing-anomalies)
(2012 working-paper page) reports that short-term reversals fail to survive costs
at reasonable institutional scale, while other styles can survive. Its live
institutional trading evidence and scale differ from a small retail implementation.
The public author-hosted summary was reviewed; no trading dataset was downloaded.

[de Groot, Huij and Zhou, Another look at trading costs and short-term reversal profits](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/25718/AnotherLook_2011.pdf)
(accepted manuscript, July 2011; Journal of Banking & Finance 36, 2012, 371-382)
reports positive net reversal returns with a large-stock universe and lower-turnover
portfolio construction. The university-hosted abstract/title-page content was reviewed.
The publisher's direct page returned 403; a successful full publisher-text review is not claimed.

These sources support competing views on implementability under costs. They do not
estimate the same object, so they are not a controlled head-to-head comparison.
They study reversal, while this pilot holds after a positive lagged return. This
pilot therefore illustrates cost sensitivity of a disclosed daily trend rule; it
neither replicates those studies nor settles their debate. It also does not measure
retail fills or intraday signals. A narrower and defensible course question is:
**Does this disclosed daily rule retain a positive return under stated cost assumptions,
and is that evidence sufficient to deploy it?**

The best available opposing evidence inside this pilot remains the positive gross
result, four positive quote-proxy sleeves, tiny modeled net loss, omitted cash yield
and exposure mismatch. The dashboard visualizes those facts. Instructor acceptance
of this narrowed controversy and WRDS source should still be confirmed.
